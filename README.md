# OEE Monitoring System

## Overview
The **Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) Monitoring System** is an industrial monitoring project designed to understand equipment efficiency and improve production productivity. It focuses on monitoring equipment performance, identifying operational losses, and supporting better industrial process management.

## Objectives
- Understand and monitor equipment effectiveness.
- Identify downtime and production losses.
- Study industrial control logic using ladder diagrams.
- Improve equipment utilization and productivity.

## OEE Calculation
OEE is calculated using three important factors:

- **Availability:** Measures the percentage of planned production time during which equipment is operating.
- **Performance:** Measures how efficiently equipment operates compared with its ideal speed.
- **Quality:** Measures the proportion of good products produced.

**Formula:**

`OEE (%) = Availability (%) × Performance (%) × Quality (%) ÷ 10,000`

This formula gives OEE as a percentage when all three factors are expressed as percentages.
